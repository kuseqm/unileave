# Lengo
Kutest mzunguko kamili wa maombi ya likizo kuanzia **staff applicant** hadi **barua ya likizo** inayotolewa.

# Magroup mawili ya testing

## Kundi 1: Watoa maombi (Applicants)
Hawa wana role ya `staff` au `dept-secretary`.
- Wanaomba leave kupitia `/leave-requests/create`.
- Wanakubali acting request ikiwa waliitwa kuwa acting.
- Mwisho wanaweza kuona/kupakua barua yao `/leave-letters`.

## Kundi 2: Waidhinishaji (Approvers)
Hawa wana roles halisi za idhini na wapo katika department/school husika.

| Hatua | Role inayohitajika | Mahitaji muhimu |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **HOD** | `hod` au `deputy-hod` | `departments.hod_staff_id` lazima awe huyo user |
| **Dean** | `dean` | `schools.dean_staff_id` lazima awe huyo user |
| **HR Review** | `hr-officer` au `hr-manager` | Same `university` na applicant, sio applicant mwenyewe |
| **DVC** | `dvca` (academic) / `dvc-pfa` (administrative) | `staff.staff_category` ya applicant iwe `academic` au `administrative` |
| **HR Logistics (mwisho)** | `hr-officer` au `hr-manager` | Same `university`, sio applicant mwenyewe |
| **Msimamizi** | `admin`, `super-admin`, `registrar` | Wanaweza kuona kila kipande |

# Misimbo ya kuona maombi (visibility)

| Role | Maombi anayoiona |
| --- | --- |
| **HOD** | Maombi ya staff wa **department yake tu** na `current_approval_level = 1` |
| **Dean** | Maombi ya staff wa **school yake tu** na `current_approval_level = 2` |
| **DVCA** | Maombi ya staff wa `staff_category='academic'` katika **university yake tu** na `current_approval_level = 4` |
| **DVCPFA** | Maombi ya staff wa `staff_category='administrative'` au `'supporting'` katika **university yake tu** na `current_approval_level = 4` |
| **HR Officer / Manager** | Maombi yote yanayokuwa level 3 au 5 katika **university yake tu** |
| **VC** | Maombi yote ya staff katika **university yake tu** |
| **Admin** | Maombi yote ya **university yake tu**; `super-admin` anakiona kila kitu |

# Jinsi ya kuandaa data kwa test

Ili kuepuka kukwama, watu watakaotest wawe katika **miundo sahihi**:

1. **Mfano wa test sahihi** (positive flow)
   - Applicant: `staff`, anayeshiriki **department X** ya **school Y**.
   - HOD: user aliyewekwa kama `departments.hod_staff_id` kwa **department X**.
   - Dean: user aliyewekwa kama `schools.dean_staff_id` kwa **school Y**.
   - HR: `hr-manager` au `hr-officer` wa same `university`.
   - DVC: `dvca` au `dvc-pfa` wa same `university`.

2. **Mfano wa test ya kutotakiwa kuona** (negative flow)
   - Tengeneza **department Z** katika **school W** nyingine.
   - Weka HOD/Dean wa department/school hiyo.
   - HOD/Dean huyu **hastakiwi kuona** maombi ya department X/school Y.
   - Hii ndio inakwenda kuthibitisha mfumo unazingatia mipaka.

3. **Vitu vya kuhakikisha kabla ya kuanza**
   - Applicant na HOD/Dean/DVC/HR wote wa **university moja**.
   - `staff_category` ya applicant imewekwa kabla ya hatua ya DVC.
   - HOD, Dean, na DVC ni **users tofauti** na applicant.

# Mzunguko wa testing hatua kwa hatua

1. **Applicant anaomba leave**
   - Ingia kama `staff`.
   - Nenda `/leave-requests/create`.
   - Chagua leave type, tarehe, acting staff, sababu.
   - Bonyeza **Submit**.

2. **Acting staff anakubali**
   - Ingia kama user aliyechaguliwa kuwa acting.
   - Nenda **Acting Requests** → **Accept**.
   - Mpaka acting haijakubaliwa, HOD hawezi kuapprove.

3. **HOD anaapprove**
   - Ingia kama `hod` (wa department ya applicant).
   - `/leave-requests/pending` → angalia badge → **Approve**.

4. **Dean anaapprove**
   - Ingia kama `dean` (wa school ya applicant).
   - `/leave-requests/pending` → **Approve**.

5. **HR Review**
   - Ingia kama `hr-officer` au `hr-manager`.
   - `/leave-requests/pending` au `/hr-review/{id}`.
   - Jaza **payment status**, **adjusted days**, **comments**, **travel allowance**.
   - Bonyeza **Approve**.
   - Hii inapeleka ombi kwa DVC.

6. **DVC anaapprove**
   - Kama applicant ni **academic**, itakwenda kwa `dvca`.
   - Kama ni **administrative**, itakwenda kwa `dvc-pfa`.
   - `/leave-requests/pending` → **Approve**.

7. **HR Logistics (hatua ya mwisho)**
   - Ingia kama `hr-officer` / `hr-manager` (mwingine tofauti na applicant).
   - Nenda `/leave-requests/hr-logistics/{id}`.
   - Jaza signature, mshahara, usafiri n.k. → **Approve**.
   - Hii hufunga maombi na kutengeneza **leave letter**.

8. **Applicant anakwenda `/leave-letters`**
   - Anaweza kuona, kupakua PDF, na kuprint.

# Muhimu ili usikwame

- **Usitumie `php artisan serve`** kwa testing wa approvals/downloads/preview; tumia **Wamp (Apache)** kwenye `http://127.0.0.1/sms_demo/public/...` au virtual host.
- **Usiweke** approver mmoja na nafasi kadhaa kwa account moja (mfano HOD na Dean)
- **Hakikisha** `departments.hod_staff_id` na `schools.dean_staff_id` zimewekwa.
- **Hakikisha** `staff.staff_category` imewekwa (`academic` au `administrative`) kabla ya DVC step.
- **Hakikisha** kila approver ni wa same `university` na applicant.
- **Usiapprove** maombi yako mwenyewe kama HR; mfumo unakataza.

# Kuwaongezea watu wapya uwezo wa kufanya test

## Njia 1: Admin UI
- Nenda **Administration → Role Management**.
- Fungua user → assign role kama `staff`, `hod`, `dean`, `hr-manager`, `dvca`, `dvc-pfa`, n.k.
- Kwa HOD/Dean pia weka `departments.hod_staff_id` / `schools.dean_staff_id`.

## Njia 2: Tinker
Fungua terminal:

```powershell
php artisan tinker
```

Kisha andika:

```php
$user = \App\Models\User::find(123);
$user->assignRole('hod');
```

Badilisha `123` na ID ya user na `hod` na role unayohitaji.

# Kutatua endapo kitu kikwama

- Angalia `storage\logs\laravel.log`.
- Kagua `current_approval_level` na `acting_status` kwenye `leave_requests` table.
- Tumia tinker kama:

```php
$req = \App\Models\LeaveRequest::find(61);
$req->update(['current_approval_level' => 1, 'acting_status' => 'accepted']);
```
