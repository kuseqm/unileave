MAELEKEZO YA KUTEST MZUNGUKO WA LEAVE APPROVAL
================================================

Lengo: Kupitia mzunguko kamili wa maombi ya likizo kutoka ombi hadi barua inapotolewa.


MAGROUP MAWILI YA TESTING
-------------------------

KUNDI A: WATOA MAOMBI (APPLICANTS)
----------------------------------
Hawa ni watu wanaoomba leave.

    Jina la kikundi: Watoa Maombi
    Roles zinazohitajika: staff au dept-secretary
    Wanaofaa kuwa hapa:
        - Staff wa kawaida
        - Dept secretary
    Wanaofanya nini:
        - Wanaomba leave kupitia /leave-requests/create
        - Wanakubali acting request ikiwa waliitwa kuwa acting
        - Mwisho wanaweza kuona/kupakua barua yao /leave-letters


KUNDI B: WAIDHINISHAJI (APPROVERS)
----------------------------------
Hawa ni watu wanaoidhinisha maombi.

    1. HOD (Head of Department)
       Jina la kikundi: HOD wa Department
       Role: hod au deputy-hod
       Anayofaa kuwa hapa:
           - Mtu aliyewekwa kama departments.hod_staff_id kwa department husika
       Maombi anayoiona:
           - Maombi ya staff wa department yake tu
           - current_approval_level lazima iwe 1

    2. DEAN
       Jina la kikundi: Dean wa School
       Role: dean
       Anayofaa kuwa hapa:
           - Mtu aliyewekwa kama schools.dean_staff_id kwa school husika
       Maombi anayoiona:
           - Maombi ya staff wa school yake tu
           - current_approval_level lazima iwe 2

    3. HR REVIEW
       Jina la kikundi: HR Review
       Roles: hr-officer au hr-manager
       Anayofaa kuwa hapa:
           - HR wa same university na applicant
           - Asiwe mwenyewe applicant
       Maombi anayoiona:
           - Maombi yote yanayokuwa level 3 katika university yake

    4. DVC
       Jina la kikundi: DVC
       Roles:
           - dvca kwa staffCategory academic
           - dvc-pfa kwa staffCategory administrative au supporting
       Anayofaa kuwa hapa:
           - DVC wa same university na applicant
           - Asiwe mwenyewe applicant
       Maombi anayoiona:
           - Maombi ya staff katika university yake tu
           - current_approval_level lazima iwe 4

    5. HR LOGISTICS (MWISHO)
       Jina la kikundi: HR Logistics
       Roles: hr-officer au hr-manager
       Anayofaa kuwa hapa:
           - HR wa same university na applicant
           - Asiwe mwenyewe applicant
       Maombi anayoiona:
           - Maombi yote yanayokuwa level 5 katika university yake

    6. MSIMAMIZI
       Jina la kikundi: Msimamizi
       Roles: admin, super-admin, au registrar
       Maombi anayoiona:
           - admin na super-admin wanaona maombi yote ya university yake
           - super-admin anakiona kila kitu


JINSI YA KUANDAA DATA KWA TEST
------------------------------

Positive flow (mfano sahihi):
    - Applicant: staff wa department X, school Y
    - HOD: user aliyewekwa kama departments.hod_staff_id kwa department X
    - Dean: user aliyewekwa kama schools.dean_staff_id kwa school Y
    - HR: hr-manager au hr-officer wa same university
    - DVC: dvca au dvc-pfa wa same university

Negative flow (mfano wa kutotakiwa kuona):
    - Tengeneza department Z, school W nyingine
    - Weka HOD/Dean wa department/school hiyo
    - HOD/Dean huyu hastakiwi kuona maombi ya department X / school Y
    - Hii inakwenda kuthibitisha mfumo unazingatia mipaka

Vitu vya kuhakikisha kabla ya kuanza:
    - Applicant na HOD/Dean/DVC/HR wote wa university moja
    - staff_category ya applicant imewekwa (academic au administrative) kabla ya DVC step
    - HOD, Dean, na DVC ni users tofauti na applicant


MZUNGUKO WA TESTING HATUA KWA HATUA
------------------------------------

1. Applicant anaomba leave
   - Ingia kama staff
   - Nenda /leave-requests/create
   - Chagua leave type, tarehe, acting staff, sababu
   - Bonyeza Submit

2. Acting staff anakubali
   - Ingia kama user aliyechaguliwa kuwa acting
   - Nenda Acting Requests -> Accept
   - Mpaka acting haijakubaliwa, HOD hawezi kuapprove

3. HOD anaapprove
   - Ingia kama hod wa department ya applicant
   - Nenda /leave-requests/pending
   - Bonyeza Approve

4. Dean anaapprove
   - Ingia kama dean wa school ya applicant
   - Nenda /leave-requests/pending
   - Bonyeza Approve

5. HR Review
   - Ingia kama hr-officer au hr-manager
   - Nenda /leave-requests/pending au /hr-review/{id}
   - Jaza payment status, adjusted days, comments, travel allowance
   - Bonyeza Approve
   - Hii inapeleka ombi kwa DVC

6. DVC anaapprove
   - Kama applicant ni academic, itakwenda kwa dvca
   - Kama ni administrative, itakwenda kwa dvc-pfa
   - Nenda /leave-requests/pending
   - Bonyeza Approve

7. HR Logistics (hatua ya mwisho)
   - Ingia kama hr-officer au hr-manager (mwingine tofauti na applicant)
   - Nenda /leave-requests/hr-logistics/{id}
   - Jaza signature, mshahara, usafiri nk
   - Bonyeza Approve
   - Hii hufunga maombi na kutengeneza leave letter

8. Applicant anakwenda /leave-letters
   - Anaweza kuona, kupakua PDF, na kuprint

-------------------

  (mfano HOD na Dean kwa user moja)

- Hakikisha staff_category imewekwa (academic au administrative) kabla ya DVC step

- Hakikisha kila approver ni wa same university na applicant

- Usiapprove maombi yako mwenyewe kama HR; mfumo unakataza

--------------------------------------------
